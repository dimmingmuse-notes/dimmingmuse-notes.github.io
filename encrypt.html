🔐 Note Encryption Tool

Encrypt a short note with a password. Everything happens in your browser - nothing is ever sent anywhere.

How it works:
  • Uses PBKDF2-SHA512 (600k iterations) to derive a key from your password; notes from the original version (200k) still decrypt
  • SHA-512 is harder for GPUs to crack than SHA-256
  • Encrypts with AES-256-GCM (includes integrity check)
  • Pads notes to a multiple of 256 bytes so the encrypted length doesn't reveal the note's length
  • Stores version + salt + IV + ciphertext in URL-safe base64
  • Decrypted notes stay blurred until you show them, and hide again after 30 seconds or when you switch away
  • Tags tab: turns tag words into private codes and back (deterministic AES-CTR + HMAC-SHA256, "SIV"), so Pinboard can search them without knowing the words
  • Optionally clears passwords and notes after a period without activity (setting above)
  • Everything happens in your browser - nothing is ever sent anywhere
